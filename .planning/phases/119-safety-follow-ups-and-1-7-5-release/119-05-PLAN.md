<objective>
Recover early per-episode import notices (IMP-03, Days of Hope incident 2026-10-09). Today `__check_webhook_imports` evaluates evidence at the ROOT level and discards a notice forever when the root is still transferring, so the first N-1 child webhooks of a season pack are lost and the coverage guard can never pass (24 deferrals, give-up).

Purpose: credit and persist a uniquely identified, fully transferred child immediately while siblings transfer; keep whole-folder deletion gated on full transfer + full coverage; survive restart (ROADMAP success criterion 5).
Output: child-credit branch, bounded pending-notice re-evaluation bound to the root's transfer lifecycle and resolved path (revalidated each cycle, discarded on USER requeue), arm-on-commit pass, immediate atomic persist (D-20), and the RED-first regression file.

Codex pass 2 (finding 2) shaped the pending-notice contract: a notice stored as a bare (root, basename) key and replayed straight into Window 2 would survive the explicit USER requeue that clears `imported_children` in CommandProcessor and would skip WebhookManager's ambiguity resolution, so a stale scan-lag notice could credit a freshly transferred, unimported replacement copy. Notices are therefore bound to a per-root lifecycle generation and the resolved child path, revalidated against the live model before either acceptance path, and discarded on USER requeue (here) and on lifecycle reset / root removal (119-07).
</objective>
