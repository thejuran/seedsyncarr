<objective>
Fix B1: make every confirmed header-swallowing consume site in the `jobs -v` parser peek before it pops, so a pget without a data line, a `\chunk` without a data line, and a mirror-empty line followed by a header can never consume the next job's header (XFER-01, D-07).

Purpose: a swallowed header means a job vanishes from status or its transfer lines get attached to the wrong job; downstream that becomes a wrong re-queue/extract/delete decision. The probe in RESEARCH showed zero existing fixtures are affected by the peek, so this is purely a narrowing fix.

Output: edited `job_status_parser.py`; commit `fix(117-04): parser peeks before popping data lines so job headers are never swallowed (B1)`.
</objective>
